## Caveats

- The sources describe facilities and intended use, not guaranteed public access. Departmental laboratories, graduate rooms, research centres, and the UMCSAWM postgraduate area may require student status, supervision, booking, or permission.
- The library source gives hours for the basement study room, but hours and access rules can change; verify with the [University of Moratuwa Library](https://uom.lk/lib) before relying on them.
- Image links above are direct URLs exposed by official UoM pages. They are provided for reuse review only; do not download, redistribute, or alter them without checking the university’s permissions/licensing.
- No claim is made that every listed place is silent, air-conditioned, or available outside teaching/research schedules; those details were not established by the cited primary sources.
